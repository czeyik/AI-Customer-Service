# AI Customer Support Chatbot — Privacy Notice Additions

Status: DRAFT for privacy-owner/legal review  
Reviewed against https://duducar.co/privacy-notice on 4 September 2026

The current Privacy Notice already mentions customer-support channels and AI-powered services.
Add the following concise text to make the WhatsApp chatbot processing, choices, providers, and
retention periods explicit. The live notice reviewed on 4 September 2026 appears to be available
only in English. Publish the complete notice—not only this chatbot addition—in both English and
Bahasa Malaysia.

## English website copy

### Add under Section I — Obtaining Personal Data

**AI customer support chatbot.** When you contact DUDU Car’s automated assistant, including
through WhatsApp, we collect your messaging identifier or telephone number, messages, selected or
detected language, and technical message details. If you ask us to create a support ticket and
give your consent, we also collect your name, email address, WhatsApp contact number, issue
description, and any relevant ride or account details or permitted supporting evidence that you
choose to provide. Please do not send
payment-card details, passwords, one-time passwords, identity documents, or information that is
not needed to resolve your issue.

### Add under Section II — Personal Data’s Usage / Customer Support

We use chatbot data to provide automated answers from DUDU Car-approved information, maintain the
conversation, detect safety or support needs, classify a requested ticket as normal, high, or
urgent, create and manage the ticket, and enable human follow-up. The assistant does not make
refunds, bookings, account changes, partnership commitments, or other decisions that change your
rights or account. A DUDU Car support representative reviews and decides the outcome of a ticket.
If you do not consent to ticket creation, no support ticket will be created and you may continue
to request general support information.

### Add under Section III — Transmission of Personal Data

We use service providers to operate the chatbot, including WhatsApp/Meta, secure hosting and
storage providers, and Z.AI acting on our instructions. Z.AI processes approved support knowledge
in Singapore to draft answers. Customer messages, names, telephone numbers, email addresses,
ticket records, identifiers, and attachments are not sent to Z.AI. We apply the safeguards
required by applicable law to this processing outside Malaysia.

### Add under Section IV — Storage of Personal Data

Chat messages are retained for **90 days from the message date**, then permanently deleted.
Support tickets and their attachments are retained for **36 months after the ticket is closed**,
then permanently deleted. We may retain particular records longer only
where required by law or under a documented and authorised legal hold.

### Add under Section VII — Your Personal Data Rights

To ask about chatbot data, withdraw ticket consent before creation, or request access, correction,
or deletion where applicable, contact **support@duducar.co** and state that your request
relates to the DUDU Car chatbot. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully
carried out and may prevent us from creating or continuing a support ticket.

## Teks laman web Bahasa Malaysia

### Tambah di bawah Bahagian I — Pemerolehan Data Peribadi

**Chatbot sokongan pelanggan AI.** Apabila anda menghubungi pembantu automatik DUDU Car, termasuk
melalui WhatsApp, kami mengumpul pengecam pemesejan atau nombor telefon, mesej, bahasa yang dipilih
atau dikesan, dan butiran teknikal mesej anda. Jika anda meminta kami membuat tiket sokongan dan
memberikan persetujuan, kami turut mengumpul nama, alamat e-mel, nombor hubungan WhatsApp,
penerangan ringkas isu, serta apa-apa butiran perjalanan atau akaun atau bukti sokongan yang
dibenarkan dan anda pilih untuk berikan. Jangan hantar
butiran kad pembayaran, kata laluan, kata laluan sekali guna, dokumen identiti, atau maklumat yang
tidak diperlukan untuk menyelesaikan isu anda.

### Tambah di bawah Bahagian II — Penggunaan Data Peribadi / Sokongan Pelanggan

Kami menggunakan data chatbot untuk memberikan jawapan automatik daripada maklumat yang
diluluskan oleh DUDU Car, mengekalkan perbualan, mengesan keperluan keselamatan atau sokongan,
mengelaskan tiket yang diminta sebagai biasa, tinggi, atau segera, membuat dan mengurus tiket,
serta membolehkan susulan oleh pegawai sokongan. Pembantu ini tidak membuat bayaran balik,
tempahan, perubahan akaun, komitmen kerjasama, atau keputusan lain yang mengubah hak atau akaun
anda. Pegawai sokongan DUDU Car akan menyemak dan menentukan hasil tiket. Jika anda tidak
bersetuju dengan pembuatan tiket, tiket sokongan tidak akan dibuat dan anda masih boleh meminta
maklumat sokongan umum.

### Tambah di bawah Bahagian III — Penghantaran Data Peribadi

Kami menggunakan penyedia perkhidmatan untuk mengendalikan chatbot, termasuk WhatsApp/Meta,
penyedia pengehosan dan storan selamat, serta Z.AI yang bertindak mengikut arahan kami. Z.AI
memproses maklumat sokongan yang diluluskan di Singapura untuk menyediakan draf jawapan. Mesej
pelanggan, nama, nombor telefon, alamat e-mel, rekod tiket, pengecam, dan lampiran tidak dihantar
kepada Z.AI. Kami menggunakan perlindungan yang dikehendaki oleh undang-undang yang terpakai bagi
pemprosesan di luar Malaysia ini.

### Tambah di bawah Bahagian IV — Penyimpanan Data Peribadi

Mesej sembang disimpan selama **90 hari dari tarikh mesej**, kemudian dipadam secara kekal. Tiket
sokongan dan lampirannya disimpan selama **36 bulan selepas tiket ditutup**, kemudian dipadam
secara kekal. Kami hanya boleh
menyimpan rekod tertentu lebih lama jika dikehendaki oleh undang-undang atau tertakluk pada
penahanan undang-undang yang didokumenkan dan diluluskan.

### Tambah di bawah Bahagian VII — Hak Data Peribadi Anda

Untuk bertanya tentang data chatbot, menarik balik persetujuan tiket sebelum tiket dibuat, atau
meminta akses, pembetulan, atau pemadaman jika berkenaan, hubungi
**support@duducar.co** dan nyatakan bahawa permintaan anda berkaitan dengan chatbot DUDU
Car. Penarikan balik persetujuan tidak menjejaskan pemprosesan yang telah dijalankan secara sah
dan mungkin menghalang kami daripada membuat atau meneruskan tiket sokongan.

Retention and privacy-contact decisions confirmed by Cze Yik on 5 September 2026: chat messages
expire 90 days after each message; tickets and attachments expire 36 months after ticket closure;
the privacy contact is `support@duducar.co`.

Provider decision confirmed by Cze Yik on 9 September 2026: Z.AI with GLM-5.3-Flash is the only
hosted pilot model; API content is processed in Singapore under Z.AI's API DPA, and only approved
support knowledge—not customer messages or identifiers—is sent.
